I've been struggling with the most frustrating random configuration setting in IntelliJ IDEA ever.

Here it is wreaking havoc on my progress during a live demo on stage:

<img src="https://api.joshlong.com/media/omg-intellij-staaaahpp.gif" width = "500" />

Infuriating!

I finally tracked it down to the following setting. NB: I'm using IntelliJ IDEA 2026.2.2.

I needed to disable the setting called `Insert the suggestion by pressing space, dot, or other context-dependent keys.`

<img src="https://api.joshlong.com/media/omg-intellij-staaaahpp-fix.png" width = "500" />

Life is so much better now!
